Privacy Policy

Redeemed Warrior Ad Studio

In plain words, Warrior

Your ads, brand profile, videos, and posters are created and stored on your own device. We never sell your information. We don't track you around the internet. This app contains no advertising networks and no trackers.

What we collect

If you create an account (optional):

  • Your email address — so you can sign in and recover your password.
  • Your password — stored only as an encrypted hash on our server. We can never read it.
  • Owner status — for the platform owner's account, a server-side role flag.

If you never create an account, nothing about you is collected at all — every feature works right on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your brand profile (business name, audience, tone, website).
  • Your saved ads ("My Ads").
  • Your poster designs, watermark settings, and social handles.
  • Your voice preferences and uploaded photos (used only in your browser).

Videos and posters are recorded by your own browser. They are never uploaded to us.

Third-party services

  • AI copywriting: when you generate an ad, your prompt is sent to our server and processed by our AI providers (Google Gemini and/or Groq) to write your copy. Prompts are not used to advertise to you.
  • AI photos: when you tap "AI Photo," a text description is sent to a free image service (Pollinations) to create your picture. No personal information is included.
  • Fonts: loaded from Google Fonts.

Sharing features

The one-tap share buttons use your phone's own share sheet. Whatever you share goes directly from your device to the app you choose (TikTok, Instagram, etc.). We never see it.

Deleting your data

Tap Account → Clear saved ads & brand profile to erase everything stored on your device instantly. To delete your server account, email us and we will remove it.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
