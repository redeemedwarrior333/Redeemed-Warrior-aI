Privacy Policy

Redeemed Warrior Devotional Bible

In plain words, Sister

Your prayers, favorites, journal notes, and progress are kept on your own device. We never sell your information. This app contains no ads and no trackers. Your time with the Word is yours alone.

What we collect

If you create an account (optional):

  • Your email address — so you can sign in across devices and recover your password.
  • Your password — stored only as an encrypted hash on our server. We can never read it.
  • Owner status — for the platform owner's account, a server-side role flag.

If you never create an account, nothing about you is collected at all — every devotional, prayer, and Bible chapter works right on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your prayer list and answered prayers.
  • Your favorite verses and devotionals.
  • Your faith challenge progress and daily reminders.
  • Your reading history.

We cannot read these even if we wanted to — they never leave your phone.

Third-party services

  • Bible text: chapters are fetched from bible-api.com in the World English Bible (public domain). Your searches are not tied to you.
  • Fonts: loaded from Google Fonts.

Deleting your data

Clearing the app's storage on your phone erases everything instantly. To delete your server account, email us and we will remove it.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Contact

Questions? Email ashleyj@godsredeemedwarrior.com and a real human (our founder, Ashley) will answer.
