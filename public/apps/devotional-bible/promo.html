Daily Devotional

Courage in the Quiet

Scripture: Psalm 46:10 (NIV) – “Be still, and know that I am God.”

Teaching: In the hustle, stillness reveals God’s gentle guidance. He speaks most clearly when we pause.

Biblical Explanation: The psalmist reminds Israel that God is a refuge, even when the earth trembles.

Real‑Life Application: Set aside five quiet minutes today. Breathe, pray, and listen for God’s heart.

Reflection Question: When was the last time silence led you to a decision?

Personal Encouragement: You are not alone; the Almighty is beside you, steady as a stone.

Closing Prayer: “Lord, help me find stillness in You. May Your peace guard my heart. Amen.”
